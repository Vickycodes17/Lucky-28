🧠 WHAT THIS ENGINE IS ACTUALLY DOING
Your race app had twenty models because a race HAS something to model — roads, slots, which vehicle has been running well. This game is three random digits added up. There is no hidden state, no form, no momentum: the chance of every number is fixed and already known, so a model that claims to feel which number is coming is selling you something.

So this engine points at the two things that can be true and would both be worth real money:

1. The draw might not be honest. If it is not, that is a far bigger edge than any mispriced pool — and it is testable. Every result you record updates a proper posterior against the exact distribution, and it will tell you the moment a number is out of line by more than luck explains.

2. The crowd might be wrong. That happens constantly, it is visible on the board every round, and it is the edge you can actually take. It needs no history at all.

It also tests, on your own results, the patterns you would be tempted by — due numbers, hot streaks, S after three B. If they are worth anything here the engine will say so. If they are not, you will see it in your own data instead of taking my word for it.

⚖️ WHY THIS GAME IS NOT THE RACE
In the race nobody knew the true chance of a vehicle, so the crowd’s guess was the best estimate going and there was nothing to beat it with. Here the true chance is arithmetic and Chamet has published it. The crowd cannot be more right than arithmetic — it can only put its money in the wrong place, and when it does, this screen says so.

The catch is the one this app cannot see: how much of a winning pot is handed to the host and guests. Measure it in the LOG tab before you trust a small edge.
